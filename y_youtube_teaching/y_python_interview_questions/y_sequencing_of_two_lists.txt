COMPLEXITY LEVEL 1  :
    You have two lists , 

    LIST_ORDER     =   [2,0,1,3,1,2]
    LIST_NAMES     =   ['RAMU' , 'RAJU' , 'SAI' , 'NARESH' , 'SITA' , 'MAX']

    Create a OUTPUT_ORDERED_LIST where the sequence in LIST_NAMES will be in the order based 
    on the values present in respective positions of LIST_ORDER.

    If the there is a duplicate in ORDER value in LIST_ORDER , 
    follow the order in which corresponding values are in the LIST_NAMES

    OUTPUT_ORDERED_LIST =   ['RAJU' , 'SAI' , 'SITA' , 'RAMU' , 'MAX' , 'NARESH']

COMPLEXITY LEVEL 2  :
    What if LIST_NAMES also have duplicates , now you cannot connect two lists with dictionary .

    LIST_ORDER     =   [2,0,1,3,1,2]
    LIST_NAMES     =   ['RAMU' , 'RAJU' , 'SAI' , 'NARESH' , 'SITA' , 'SAI']


    OUTPUT_ORDERED_LIST =   ['RAJU' , 'SAI' , 'SITA' , 'RAMU' , 'SAI' , 'NARESH']
#### QUESTION ## END #########################################################




#### ANSWERS ## START ########################################################

## For COMPLEXITY LEVEL 1 :

LIST_ORDER     =   [2,0,1,3,1,2]
LIST_NAMES     =   ['RAMU' , 'RAJU' , 'SAI' , 'NARESH' , 'SITA' , 'MAX']

DICT_TO_CONNECT = {}

for i in range(0,len(LIST_ORDER)):
    DICT_TO_CONNECT[LIST_NAMES[i]] = LIST_ORDER[i]

print(DICT_TO_CONNECT)
