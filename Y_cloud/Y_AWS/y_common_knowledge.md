    IAM :

        2) Key points :
                Users       : A person who gets authenticated through "USERNAME and PASSWORD" .
                GROUPS      : A collection of USERS based on their job or any other metric .
                ROLES       : Either an USER or an ENTITY which doesnt require PASSWORD for authentication
